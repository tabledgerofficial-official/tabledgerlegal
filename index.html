Privacy Policy

TabLedger · Effective date: October 21, 2026

1. About this policy

TabLedger is a ledger app for shop owners. It keeps track of customers and the money they owe. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

2. Information the app stores

You may enter the following into the app:

• Your shop name, phone number and address.

• Your customers' names, phone numbers and notes.

• Credits, payments, dates and amounts.

3. Where your information is kept

• Your information is stored only on your device.

• We do not have a server. We do not receive, see or store your data.

• You do not need an account to use the app.

4. What we do not do

• We do not sell your information.

• We do not share your information with advertisers.

• We do not use advertising or analytics tools in the app.

5. Notifications

The app can send you reminders showing the total amount owed and how many customers owe. These are created on your device. You can turn them off in the app or in your phone settings.

6. Sharing receipts

When you share a receipt, you choose the app and the person to send it to. The receipt is then handled by that app under its own privacy rules. We do not see it.

7. Backups

• You can export a backup file of your data. You decide where to save it and who can access it.

• Your phone may also include app data in its own backup, such as Google backup. That is controlled by your phone and Google settings.

8. Customer information

You are responsible for the information you enter about your customers. Please keep it accurate and use it only for your business.

9. Security

Your data stays on your device, so please protect your phone with a lock screen. If you lose your phone and have no backup, your data may be lost.

10. Children

TabLedger is meant for business use. It is not intended for children.

11. Deleting your data

You can delete customers inside the app. Uninstalling the app removes its data from your device. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The new date will appear at the top.
